Privacy

This notice describes the native easygoae app in its current TEST mode.

Data processed by the app

You may enter practice and bank details, patient details, treatments, estimates, invoices, payments and stock. The app uses these details locally for the billing and management functions you choose.

Storage on your Mac

The library is a SQLite database in your macOS user account's private app container. It also contains cached PDFs. Before certain library upgrades, the app creates a local backup. Language and appearance are stored in macOS app preferences.

No automatic transmission by the app

The app has no cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics services and requires no developer account. It does not automatically send the library data you enter to the developer. External links you choose to open are handled by your browser.

Imports, exports and backups

You choose the library to import and the destination for PDF, CSV, JSON or SQLite exports. Files placed in synchronized folders or backups may be sent to other services according to your macOS or cloud settings. The app does not control those services. It adds no separate encryption to its database or exports.

Retention and deletion

The app does not send a copy of your library to the developer. Local data remains while the library exists. Archiving hides records and does not delete them. Removing the app does not reliably remove its library, separate exports or backups. Contact the developer below for questions about removing local data; the developer cannot remotely access or delete your library.

Apple services

The App Store, TestFlight, macOS diagnostics and device backups you configure are operated by Apple or your chosen providers. Their privacy notices and your settings apply to those services.

Contacting the developer

App publisher: Oguz Kaan Kahraman. Use the public GitHub tracker linked below for technical questions. Posting requires a GitHub account; your username and message will be public. GitHub privacy and deletion controls also apply to that content. Do not post patient records, bank details or other confidential information. The app does not send your library to this tracker.

These help pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They include no analytics or advertising code added by us, external fonts or contact form. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed into GitHub. See GitHub's privacy statement linked below for details.

GitHub privacy statement

Technical support

The support tracker is public. Describe the problem, app version, macOS version and steps to reproduce it. Use fictional examples. Do not send patient records, invoices, databases, bank details or screenshots containing personal information.
